## Experience

**Senior Researcher, Advanced Computing** — [LINKS Foundation](https://linksfoundation.com/en/) · Torino, Italy · 2021 – present

- **Co-coordinator of CLEAR-EO** (Horizon Europe) and WP3 lead; proposals for Horizon Europe, EuroHPC and national projects.
- **Lagrange** — operations lead of Italy's first publicly-accessible quantum computer (IQM Spark), open-source management stack; first hybrid HPC–QC job fully run in Italy, with Leonardo.
- **Quantum–HPC integration** — hybrid scheduling (Slurm, QPU sharing); co-lead of EQS3 working groups.
- **Quantum algorithms** — QUBO/MIS on neutral atoms and annealers; QEC4QEA deputy WP3 lead.
- **Infrastructure** — internal services (GitLab, Nextcloud, JupyterHub), scaled from group to institute-wide.

**Research Engineer** — [Noesis Solutions](https://www.noesissolutions.com) · Novara, Italy · 2015 – 2021

- **ML methodologies for engineering**; numerical code and software stack.
- Source code management redesign; technical contact for funded projects.
